# Tactical Reproduction Scoring Criteria

---

## 1. Total Points Structure

There are a total of **6 tactics** in this task, ranging from A001 to A006 (A000 does not need to be answered). Each question is divided into two sub-scores:

| Item | Max Points per Question | Scoring Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Drawing Positioning** | 25 | Indicate the role, position, and passing method for each catch on the tactical board. |
| **Executing Tactics** | 75 | Execute the tactic physically on the field. |

**Total: 600 points** (100 points/question × 6 questions).

---

## 2. Tactic Description

The tactic description only records each catch.

| Element | Definition | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Node (點)** | The receiver and their catching position. | Attacking Midfielder (AM) catches the ball at the **right half-space**. |
| **Edge (邊)** | The passing method prior to that beat. | **Pass**, **Through ball**, **Cross**, **Cut-back pass**, etc. |
| **Result (if any)** | The ending state of that beat. | **Shot on target**, **Goal**, **Offside**, etc. |

Tactic description format:

Full-back (FB) catches the ball on the right flank (rear section).
FB passes to the Attacking Midfielder (AM), AM catches the ball at the right half-space.
AM crosses to the Striker (ST), ST scores in the center of the penalty area.

---

## 3. Position Determination

Both Drawing Positioning and Executing Tactics are based on the **20 tactical zones (zone 1–20)**, which correspond to the blue grids on the field (see the tactical board).

---

## 4. Defending Players on the Field

* There are approximately **9** stationary defending players configured on the field.
* The defending players **do not move throughout the entire process**; the line connecting them forms the defensive line.

---

## 5. Drawing Positioning (25 points/question)

### Answering Requirements

1. Answer according to the **tactic description** provided in the coach's area.
2. Sequentially indicate **every catch** on the tactical board:
* **Receiver** (must indicate the role, e.g., FB, AM, ST)
* **Catching position**
* **The passing method prior to that beat** (Edge; for the first beat, only the receiver and position need to be indicated)
* Which beat the catch occurs on
* Indicate the question number in the top-left corner.



A fully correct drawn positioning for a question earns 25 points. For an example, please see the A000 example in the coach's area.

---

## 6. Executing Tactics (75 points/question)

The tactical content for Executing Tactics is the same as Drawing Positioning, but it is executed physically on the field. It includes the following rules:

1. The receiver must touch the ball on the correct beat and in the correct zone.
2. All catches and shots specified in the tactical language must be completed within 10 seconds.
3. The passer cannot leave their own zone to pass to the next person.
4. Before passing, the ball must be on the ground and must be in the correct zone.
5. The ball leaves the football field area.

If any of the above rules are violated, the attempt is considered a failure. You have a total of 3 attempts.
